Planned · Milestone 2 · Ask nimbls site knowledge

A network question?
Have a clear answer ready.

Your customer or manager asks, “Was the network stable last night? Is anything still being followed up?” Turn the available site records into a concise answer, with evidence behind it.

YOU ASK“My customer wants an overnight network update. Draft a short reply with any recorded issues and what still needs checking.”
WHAT YOU COULD RECEIVEFictional example

A reply you can review and share

  • What we observed: repeated disconnect events on one switch during the reviewed period.
  • What we’re checking: whether the events line up with the maintenance window.
  • What we can’t confirm yet: customer impact and recovery are not established by the available records.

Supporting references: the reviewed time range, source events, and available device records.

Planned site-knowledge capability. Review the draft before sharing; nimbls does not automatically send the reply. Missing evidence stays explicit.

Find what changed.
Know what to check.

A switch keeps disconnecting after maintenance. Instead of searching through separate records, bring its device history and recent events into one investigation.

YOU ASK“This switch has been disconnecting since yesterday’s maintenance. What changed, and what should I check?”
