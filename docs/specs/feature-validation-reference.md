# Feature validation reference

Planning criteria retained from the marketing source catalog. These are engineering/design criteria, not customer-facing sales copy.

## Custom Agents

The saved agent survives restart and reuses the requested instructions; evaluate the report against known input records.

## Site History & Knowledge

Seed known historical events, retrieve them across executions, and check source/time references. Missing evidence remains explicit.

## Local AI

Complete both representative workflows with no external inference requests; record supported hardware, model, and observed limitations.

## Scheduling & Automation

Verify daily dispatch, fresh sessions, overlap skips, no catch-up, schedule controls, Stop, and Quit.

## Results Center

Verify search matches period/subject, report interactions stay isolated, and archived files remain retrievable under the stated policy.

## Agent Improvement Review

Show whether the target case improves and whether known-good cases regress; retain input/configuration context for comparison.

## Controlled External Assistance

Demonstrate both allowed and blocked requests, the exact outgoing payload/destination, configured cost limits, and local verification.

## Execution Management

Prove scope enforcement, actual-state verification, and truthful partial-failure handling on a defined supported operation.
